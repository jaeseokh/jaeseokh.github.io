Research, code, and decision analytics

Jaeseok Hwang

Ph.D. candidate in Applied Economics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

My work studies decision risk under price, production, weather, and market uncertainty. I build empirical workflows with Python, R, SQL, machine learning, econometrics, and reproducible reporting to evaluate when model-based recommendations remain reliable outside the setting where they were trained.

This page collects my research repositories and keeps the Treasury regime terminal as a separate live research venue for fixed-income regime detection and market-plumbing monitoring.
